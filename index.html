WHO WE ARE

An Industrial Business Built Around Supply & Manufacturing

Third Eye Minerals & Processors Private Limited is an Odisha-based industrial company involved in mineral processing, mineral trading, cement manufacturing and building material supply.

From sized iron ore and fines to coal, cement, AAC blocks, bricks and pavers, the company serves a broad range of industrial and construction requirements.

With a monthly capacity of up to 1,00,000 metric tonnes and supply operations across Odisha, the company focuses on dependable sourcing, precision processing and timely delivery.
